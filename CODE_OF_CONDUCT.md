# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants should communicate respectfully, critique evidence rather than people, disclose conflicts, and avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or publication of private information.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related communication. Report conduct concerns privately to `litaishan@caf.ac.cn`. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or escalate serious matters to the relevant institution.

Good-faith scientific disagreement, requests for reproducibility evidence, and careful reporting of limitations are encouraged.
