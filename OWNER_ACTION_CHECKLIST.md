# Owner action checklist

The following items require an author/rightsholder decision and cannot be completed by repository automation.

- [ ] Confirm the final manuscript author list, order, affiliations, ORCIDs, and corresponding author.
- [ ] Confirm CRediT contributions, funding, acknowledgments, and competing-interest language.
- [ ] Identify the code/documentation copyright holder and approve an SPDX license.
- [ ] Approve a model-weight distribution route and verify base-model license compatibility.
- [ ] Approve internal image/annotation access terms and institutional privacy review.
- [ ] Reconfirm the exact licenses and versions of all external datasets.
- [ ] Decide whether reviewer access remains private, uses an anonymous snapshot, or becomes public at submission.
- [ ] After all P0 gates pass, authorize the stable tag, GitHub Release, archival DOI, and manuscript availability update.
